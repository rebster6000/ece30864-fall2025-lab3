1. Pros vs Cons of Agile Development
    Pros:
        -The iterative approach helps development happen faster and allows the product to be launched quicker
        -Constant colaboration with the customer helps ensure customer satisfaction and minimizes having to redo work.
    Cons:
        -Agile development takes more time and commitment from both the team and the team leader ith frequent communication and updates.
        -Less thorough documentation can make future development harder and lead to problems down the line.


2. Roles and Responsabilities of Scrum Master vs Product Owner
    Scrum Master: In charge of all scrum related tasks which includes JIRA tooling and facilitating sprints and meetings.
    Prodcut Owner: In charge of setting the direction for the team and also handles release and stakeholder management.


3. 3 Types of Meetings During a Sprint Cycle
    -Sprint Planning Meeting
        -Determine what to complete in the upcoming sprint
        -Determine story points and assign stories to teams
    -Daily Scrum/Standup Meeting
        -Team members discuss work completed, 
        -Team members discuss work plan for the day and any potential roadblocks
    -Sprint Retrospective Meeting  
        -Discuss work completed
        -Discuss what work the move to the backlog and what work to continue on
